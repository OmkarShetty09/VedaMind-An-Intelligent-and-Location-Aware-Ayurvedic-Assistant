## About Ayurwiki

Ayurwiki is an open, community-driven encyclopedia of Ayurveda, documenting herbs,
medicines, practices, yoga, and traditional wisdom. It was awarded Gold under the
"Award for Excellence in Information Technology in AYUSH sector - 2019".
